Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Skipora is a local-first jump rope practice logger for iPhone and iPad. Practice sessions, sets, rest rows, notes, photos, and Skips balances stay in the app sandbox on this device. Skipora does not operate an account server or a private cloud sync backend for your practice records.

Who we are

Skipora is published as a personal practice tool. Questions go through in-app Support on the device.

Data stored on this device

  • Session titles, start and end times, time zones, status values, tags, and notes you enter.
  • Practice and rest set rows, jump counts (including unknown counts), timing modes, pause intervals, and derived active durations.
  • Optional reference photos you take or pick, stored as local copies in the app sandbox.
  • A local one-tap session identifier so the app can return you to the practice desk.
  • Skips balance, ledger entries, and Apple StoreKit transaction identifiers after a purchase is verified.
  • Export archives you generate, only while you create them; you choose where to save them with the system share sheet.

Login

Sign-in is one tap on this device. Skipora does not offer Sign in with Apple and does not collect a username or password. Log Out ends the local session and leaves practice records and Skips on the device. Delete Account removes the local session identifier and the user data written by that session on this installation, including practice vault files, local media copies, and the Skips wallet.

Permissions

  • Camera is requested only after you choose Take Photo when attaching an optional session photo. You can decline and still save text records or use Choose Photos.
  • Choose Photos is used when you pick an existing image to copy into a practice session on this device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition are requested only after you tap Dictate Note while writing a set feel note, so spoken words can become editable text. You can decline and type the note instead.
  • App Tracking Transparency may be requested after you reach the main practice desk, so Skipora can understand which features you open most. Session photos and notes are not sold or used for ads. You can decline.
  • Push notification permission may be requested by the system after the main desk loads. Skipora does not request location, contacts, HealthKit, Bluetooth, or calendar in this version.

Purchases

Skips are Apple consumable in-app purchases. Apple processes the payment. A new local session may receive a starter Skips balance on this device; that gift is not an App Store product. Skips live on this device and are not restored after you delete the app or use Delete Account. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Starter content

Starter practice sessions shown after install are local on-device content for interface review. They are not a live networked social feed and are not shared with other users.

Sharing and export

Records leave the device only when you start an export and save or share the archive through the system share sheet. Skipora does not upload practice notes or photos to a Skipora business server.

Retention and deletion

You can archive or permanently delete a session in the app, or Delete Account in Settings. Those actions remove the matching local records and unreferenced media files on this installation. Files you already exported elsewhere are not recalled by the app.

Contact

Use in-app Support on the device.
